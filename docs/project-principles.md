# Project principles

Status: Established — user’s initial design brief in **Game Design Rules Balance**, with later explicit revisions noted below. Source: [S0](../migration/source-index.md).

## Simulation and agency

The player controls one primary character at a time. The world continues independently, with persistent NPC goals, relationships, careers, families, injuries and deaths. Choices influence it without making the protagonist its automatic center.

Do not grant automatic talent, popularity, bloodlines, canon relationships, victory or destiny. Failure, rejection, permanent consequences and death are possible. Difficulty must remain fair and understandable. Important choices belong to the player; provide roughly 3–6 suggestions when useful and always allow a custom action.

Begin character creation interactively: era, birthplace/village, gender, name, family preference and amount of randomness. Support Full Random, Guided Random and Custom origins. No creation choices have been made in this migration.

## Consistency

Capabilities, preparation, information, environment, cost and uncertainty determine outcomes. The player and NPCs use the same mechanical principles. Important fights use meaningful phases or rounds; tactical choices and incomplete enemy information matter.

Persist resources, injuries, inventories, relationships and consequences. Consumed or lost objects do not reappear. Advancement takes years, with diminishing returns and recovery needs; rank is not a universal power score.

## Information

Separate objective truth, character knowledge and belief. Reveal hidden traits, secrets and distant events only through an appropriate information channel. Hidden rolls normally remain hidden; explanations can expose relevant known factors without exposing secret facts.

The user explicitly authorized GitHub storage of hidden information in S0's later persistence discussion. Hidden means omitted from normal gameplay; the repository owner can still inspect the files.

## A life across generations

Track time and age; choose turn duration to suit childhood, Academy life, missions or downtime. Maintain families and allow a suitable successor after retirement or death. Relationships and NPC behavior persist rather than being rewritten to accommodate the player.

Money, work, missions, expenses, politics and civilian life matter without tedious bookkeeping. World events arise from state. News reaches the character through rumors, conversations, briefings or announcements.

## Setting and creative freedom

Use Naruto's world as the starting foundation; allow original people, organizations and believable alternate history. Do not force canon events after the timeline has diverged.

The user explicitly permits non-canon jutsu and techniques when coherent with established principles (S3, turn 3045d7a7-087f-4d0c-90e9-79021962b01e). Permission does not grant any particular technique to a character.

## Scope

These are project requirements, not finished combat, economy or relationship algorithms. Their detailed mechanics remain Open outside Rulesets 1–3. Earlier attribute/skill lists in the initial brief are Deprecated where replaced by [Ruleset 1](../rules/01-character-growth/README.md).
