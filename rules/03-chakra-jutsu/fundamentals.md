# Chakra fundamentals

Source: S1 foundational revision; S3 section 3.1. Long-message tail is incomplete; see [migration coverage](../../migration/README.md).

## Established — separate concepts

Chakra is a foundational attribute. Chakra Reserves are the usable resource. Chakra Control is learned precision and efficiency. Large reserves do not automatically imply strong control or unrestricted technique output.

Spent resources remain spent until a causal recovery or transfer occurs. The user’s simulation brief already requires persistent chakra and consequences of exhaustion. No encounter-end refill is implied.

## Provisional — developed resource model

Track current/maximum reserves in internal Chakra Points (CP). Maximum reserves depend chiefly on Chakra, maturation, training, physiology and traits, with Endurance supporting rather than replacing Chakra.

Two characters with the same Chakra attribute can have different developed reserves and applications. Young characters need not possess their eventual adult capacity.

The section proposes these approximate strain bands:

| Draft state | Draft reserve percentage |
|---|---|
| Normal | 50–100% |
| Taxed | 25–50% |
| Low | 10–25% |
| Critical | 1–10% |
| Exhausted | 0 CP |

Shared endpoints and values between 0% and 1% require clarification. This table is narrative tuning, not executable classification.

Zero CP does not automatically mean unconsciousness in the proposal. Normal chakra use becomes unavailable; physical capability depends on condition. Drawing beyond safe reserves is dangerous overexertion, not a guaranteed heroic power-up. Injury, collapse or death must follow defined exposure and consequences.

## Provisional — efficiency and recovery

A technique has a useful energy requirement and can waste chakra through poor control. Efficiency has a technique-specific floor; mastery never implies universally free techniques.

Costs may include activation, maintenance and scaling. Recovery depends on Chakra, Endurance, rest, health, nutrition and circumstances. Chakra and physical stamina are related but distinct. Injury can affect maximum capacity, recovery, control or safe output differently.

Foreign and stored chakra need origin, availability, compatibility and handling constraints. Their presence does not automatically make them safely usable. Chakra signatures and estimates of others' reserves belong to information rules, not omniscient player displays.
