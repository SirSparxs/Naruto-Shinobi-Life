# Chakra Control

## Established — expanded progression

Sources: S1's explicit skill revision; S3 user turn 0ec3d8cd-9747-40f3-9f52-1241ed2748c7 and subsequent continuation.

Chakra Control is one of the most important skills. Its current structure is:

| Level | Tiers | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Basic | I–V | 5 |
| Intermediate | I–V | 5 |
| Advanced | I–V | 5 |
| Expert | I–V | 5 |
| Master | I–VII | 7 |
| Total | | 27 |

Store progress within each tier on the common 0–100 scale. Call it **tier progress** to distinguish it from mastery of a specific jutsu; the source reply sometimes used “mastery” for both.

This specific track supersedes the earlier simple five-level Control progression and takes precedence over ordinary skill-tier examples.

## Provisional — qualitative development

Basic concerns conscious manipulation; Intermediate reliable shinobi application; Advanced refined manipulation and specialization; Expert exceptional control; Master increasingly rare precision and efficiency.

Level transitions are proposed as greater qualitative thresholds than refinement between tiers within a level. Exact unlock requirements, XP/progress costs and capabilities at every tier remain Open.

Control supports efficiency, precision, stability, suppression, sustained effects, multitasking and pressure management. It does not grant elemental conversion, medical knowledge, shape-transformation mastery or unfamiliar jutsu without learning them.

Separate hard prerequisites from difficulty. Some actions cannot be attempted without the required method or knowledge; others are possible but unreliable. Do not turn high Control into universal access.

More reserves or output may make delicate control harder. Foreign chakra, injury, stress and disruption can affect specific processes. Fine manipulation and gross reinforcement are different applications. Efficiency floors remain technique-specific.

## Open — completion

Define all 27 tiers' unlock requirements and their relationship to the resolution scale. Tune costs and limits for simultaneous processes. Resolve learning and skill-specific gates without adding unapproved powers to upper Master tiers.

Examples such as advanced medical work or storage techniques illustrate potential applications; reaching a tier does not automatically teach them. [Issue #3](https://github.com/SirSparxs/Naruto-Shinobi-Life/issues/3).
