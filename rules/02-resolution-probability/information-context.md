# Information, context and complex actions

Status: Established conceptual principles from the continued S2 discussion; exact utility, progress and modifier equations are Provisional/Open.

## Information

Maintain objective truth separately from each character's knowledge, beliefs, confidence and sources. Observation does not automatically provide correct interpretation. Conceal the existence of a hidden check when revealing it would itself expose a secret.

Characters estimate difficulty, capability, odds and stakes from their information. NPCs choose according to their goals, values, personality, relationships and perceived risk, not engine omniscience. Personality affects choices, not physical probability.

Narrate observable outcomes and major known causes. Ordinary gameplay uses qualitative judgments. Mechanical explanations must not reveal undiscovered facts. Perceived confidence is not a promise of success.

## Resources, time and stress

Resources help only when their expenditure changes an actual mechanism: precision, output, duration, speed, safety or flexibility. No generic “10 chakra buys +10 success.” More chakra can exceed a technique's useful operating range.

Taking time can improve some actions but introduces exposure, opportunity cost or stale information. Stress affects relevant functions, such as fine control or planning; it is not automatically a penalty to everything. Experience and mastery help under familiar pressure.

Luck cannot replace prerequisites or make impossible actions possible. Critical events emerge from extreme outcomes plus a real opportunity/hazard, not a universal natural-1/natural-20 rule.

## Teams and projects

Select a teamwork structure appropriate to the task: lead/support, weakest link, collective effort, best specialist, parallel roles or synergy. Do not add two characters' skill scores into a fictitious super-character.

Extended actions preserve progress, knowledge, quality, resources, setbacks and milestones. Scope is amount of work; difficulty is technical challenge. Repeated checks alone are not a project model.

Progression depends on meaningful challenge, feedback, novelty, participation and adaptation. Emit learning events to Ruleset 1 rather than awarding growth per roll. Trivial grinding, intentional failure and impossible-task farming do not generate free development.
