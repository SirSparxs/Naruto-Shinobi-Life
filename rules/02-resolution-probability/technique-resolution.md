# Ruleset 2 interfaces with completed Ruleset 3

Status: Provisional integration of developed Ruleset 3 mechanics. Established general safeguards remain in [resolution](resolution.md). Sources: Ruleset 3.6–35, linked by [coverage](../03-chakra-jutsu/coverage.md).

Ruleset 3 defines what a technique does and requires. Ruleset 2 resolves genuine uncertainty. Ruleset 1 evaluates learning/growth. No universal combat damage, action duration or multi-skill formula is introduced here.

## 1. Feasibility before probability

Check the actual method: biology, learned prerequisites, equipment, material, contract, access, delivery vector, target/geometry and compatible resources. Distinguish a hard prerequisite from a recommended soft threshold. A technique-specific alternate method or dangerous overexertion can enable an attempt only when explicitly supported.

Having sufficient total CP is not sufficient Output, Control or concentration. A reservoir behind a suppressing seal still exists but may be inaccessible. An absorber with free capacity may nevertheless lack the rate to intercept an incoming attack. Where quantity, rate or compatibility is uncertain, resolve that specific uncertainty rather than granting automatic immunity or a generic bonus.

## 2. Execution stages, cost and interruption

Follow the technique's actual sequence: preparation, seals/physical components, chakra gathering and shaping, targeting, release, maintenance. Not every technique has all stages.

Handsign Speed affects physical sequencing; it is not total activation time, projectile velocity or reaction capacity. One-handed/seal-less use needs its own supported method. Enhanced senses supply information, not automatically fast enough bodily responses.

Record chakra invested, locked, divided, consumed or returned according to the technique at each relevant stage. Early interruption, late interruption and successful release can have different resource/backlash results. Failure need not refund cost. A successfully launched attack need not vanish when its caster is interrupted afterward.

Do not require a separate roll for every stage. Check only distinct consequential uncertainty not already resolved. Combat scheduling and exact time windows belong to the combat rules; this interface supplies constraints.

## 3. Contests and simultaneous outcomes

Use one shared resolution when actors directly contest the same objective. Two projectiles that do not intercept may both succeed. Partial penetration, mutual destruction, deflection, merger and residual effects are valid clash results when the mechanisms allow them.

Classify hard, soft or situational counters by actual mechanism. Check timing, coverage, geometry, nature, shape, stability, local pressure and relevant Output; rank alone never decides. An ordinary elemental advantage is not universally a hard counter. A genuine hard counter still needs its access and delivery conditions.

Automatic outcomes require actual certainty or incompatibility, not a label. Sustained clashes track resource and stability changes; they are not free repeated rolls against unchanged state.

## 4. Resource conservation and persistent consequences

Keep personal, stored, external, beast and Senjutsu pools identifiable. Track source, ownership, allocation, access, conversion loss, throughput and recovery. Absorption moves or processes chakra; disruption need not grant chakra; suppression need not destroy it. Natural Energy is not interchangeable with ambient ordinary chakra.

Clone division/return, transfer, summoning and sealing cannot duplicate net finite supply without a defined external source or creation mechanism. Material components are consumed, retained or recovered according to their role. Merely knowing a storage or summoning method does not create contents or an entity.

Sustained, tethered, autonomous, self-sustaining and materialized effects have different ending conditions. Loss of consciousness, death, range or concentration ends only what depends on that condition. Completed independent seals, released matter, injuries, fires and altered terrain can persist. Removing chakra is not automatically removal of resulting physical hazards.

Mastery can reduce avoidable error but cannot erase an intrinsic sacrifice or make treatment biologically impossible under the chosen method become possible.

## 5. Knowledge and interpretation

Persist observation separately from identification, understanding and learned execution. Sensing or dōjutsu reveals only its supported properties. Genjutsu recognition does not itself dispel it; correct diagnosis does not itself heal; recording a bloodline technique does not create its biology.

Intentional exploitation of a hidden weakness requires knowledge. The weakness may still causally affect an interaction without either character understanding why. Narration exposes the character's evidence, not engine truth or the existence of a secret roll.

Summons and tailed beasts retain their own agency, memories, knowledge, resource pools and relationships. Contract/cooperation is not an automatic additive skill bonus.

## 6. Learning and world updates

Emit learning evidence with method, recipient, technique/version, relevant feedback, novelty, source and any clone transfer/assimilation context. Ruleset 1 determines advancement; no skill XP per roll, copied mastery or automatic research success.

Off-screen abstraction uses the same prerequisites, knowledge and resources. Persist consequential consumption, injury, revealed information, new techniques and terrain changes. Generate an NPC's undefined details from established history before use, never after a player's choice to manufacture a counter.
