# Ruleset 7 — NPCs, Relationships & Social Interactions
## Simulation Reference Summary

**Purpose:** Ruleset 7 governs NPC social behavior, relationships, social influence, memory, reputation, romance, information flow, social access, off-screen social simulation, and the social consequences of events. It does **not** replace Rulesets 1–4 or future institutional/economic/legal systems.

### 1. Core Social Principles
- NPCs are autonomous people, not player-centered content. PCs and NPCs follow the same social rules.
- Social influence changes understanding, incentives, emotion, willingness, or behavior only within plausible limits. Mundane social skills never become mind control.
- Relationships are directional and multidimensional. A→B can differ from B→A.
- No universal relationship score. Core dimensions include **Trust, Respect, Affection/Attachment, Loyalty, Fear, Familiarity, Obligation/Indebtedness, Resentment, Hostility, Romantic Interest, and adult Sexual Chemistry**.
- **No Cross-Dimension Purchase:** Affection cannot buy Trust; Trust cannot buy attraction; Loyalty cannot erase Fear; Romantic Interest or Sexual Chemistry cannot buy consent.
- NPCs act on what they **believe**, not objective truth. Knowledge, belief, suspicion, rumor, and misbelief must remain distinct.
- Social state is usually hidden. The player learns through dialogue, behavior, context, evidence, and Insight—not exposed meters.
- Not every interaction requires a roll. If the outcome is obvious, resolve directly.
- Failed attempts change context. Repeating the same request without meaningful change does not grant free rerolls.
- Player voluntary choices remain player-controlled. NPC mundane social skills cannot force them.

### 2. NPC Simulation & Identity
- NPC simulation tiers: **Major, Supporting, Persistent Minor, Background**. Tier controls detail, not power.
- Promote/demote NPC detail as relevance changes while preserving established facts.
- NPC identity should include only the depth needed: biography, role, culture, personality, values, biases, communication style, important relationships, goals, and relevant romantic/adult baseline.
- Procedural NPCs must come from plausible lives. Generate context/role/history before skills and details.
- High skill, rank, rare ability, trauma, secret, or exceptional status requires a plausible cause. Do not make every NPC special or dramatic.
- Adults should usually have plausible networks, routines, coworkers, family, friends, mentors, rivals, etc.
- Canon provides baseline; causal simulation may diverge and persistent project history then becomes authoritative.

### 3. Goals, Agency & Decision-Making
- NPC decisions draw from identity, goals, beliefs, memories, relationships, emotions, physical state, risk, authority, commitments, and context.
- Goals and motives can conflict. NPCs satisfice rather than perfectly optimize.
- Hard boundaries, values, moral red lines, fear, duty, and loyalty can restrict available choices.
- Emotions influence decisions but do not dictate them.
- Rejection, refusal, dissent, and disagreement are valid outcomes.
- NPCs can initiate requests, friendships, confrontations, romance, apologies, favors, breakups, reconciliation, defection, etc. without player prompting.

### 4. Knowledge, Beliefs, Secrets & Memory
- Track objective truth separately from each character’s belief.
- Useful belief states: **Known, Believed, Suspected, Uncertain, Disbelieved, Unknown, Misbelieved**; confidence is separate from accuracy.
- Information should preserve source/provenance when important.
- Secrets are compartmentalized; one person learning a secret does not make it public.
- Memory is imperfect. Salient, emotional, repeated, relationship-defining, promise-related, betrayal-related, or identity-relevant events persist more strongly.
- Routine repeated events may compress into pattern memories.
- Reinterpretation may change what a memory means without changing what objectively happened.
- Insight is not a lie detector or mind reading. It interprets cues and inconsistencies.

### 5. Emotions & Relationship State
- Temporary emotional state is separate from personality and long-term relationship dimensions.
- Emotions may include anger, fear, grief, joy, shame, guilt, jealousy, affection, anxiety, excitement, disgust, loneliness, confidence, suspicion, curiosity, and adult arousal.
- Mixed emotions are valid.
- Adult arousal is temporary, adult-only, and separate from attraction, romance, initiation, or consent.
- Relationship labels such as friend, rival, sibling, mentor, partner, ex-partner, teammate, or commander describe structure; they do not determine exact dimensions.
- Relationship history, trajectory, stability, unresolved conflict, promises, and expectations matter.
- Relationship change must be causal, proportional, dimension-specific, and resistant to grinding. Major betrayal or sacrifice can cause large changes; routine contact usually causes small changes.

### 6. Social Context, Skills & Resolution
- Social context may include privacy, audience, status/rank, legitimacy, urgency, emotion, physical state, location, evidence, leverage, stakes, goals, relationship history, reputation, witnesses, power imbalance, coercion, authority, romance, and prior attempts.
- Core social skills: **Persuasion, Negotiation, Deception, Insight, Etiquette, Leadership, Intimidation, Teaching, Interrogation, Performance, Diplomacy, adult Seduction**.
- There is **no Charisma attribute**. Social effectiveness = relevant skill + relevant attribute + context + relationship + evidence + reputation + leverage + plausibility.
- Social intent comes first; skill is the method. Common intents include request, persuade, negotiate, comfort, befriend, impress, recruit, command, deceive, gather information, intimidate, interrogate, de-escalate, apologize, reconcile, flirt, court, seduce (18+), or end a relationship.
- Standard social procedure: **Intent → Desired Outcome → Possibility Check → Baseline Willingness/Resistance → Need for Roll? → Context → Skill/Attribute → Opposition → Ruleset 2 Resolution → NPC Response → Relationship/Memory/Knowledge/Reputation Consequences → Persist changed context.**
- Possibility is checked before probability. Impossible outcomes do not receive harder rolls.
- Degrees of success improve the best plausible outcome; exceptional success never overrides agency, reality, or institutional limits.

### 7. Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation & Interrogation
- Persuasion works only where genuine reconsideration is possible. Deep values and identity usually change through repeated evidence/experience, not one roll.
- Deception changes belief, not reality. Track target belief, evidence, contradictions, and lie maintenance.
- Successful Deception should actually mislead the player if no contrary evidence is available; failed Deception may create suspicion without revealing exact truth.
- Intimidation uses credible fear. Fear may produce compliance, resistance, flight, freezing, deception, resentment, or later retaliation.
- Authority does not equal loyalty. Commands work within legitimate role/authority boundaries.
- Coercion and blackmail restrict choice and must remain tagged as coercive; compliance is not genuine willingness.
- Interrogation can retrieve only what the target knows, believes, remembers, guesses, or fabricates. False confessions are possible. Torture is not a truth machine.

### 8. Reputation, Rumors, Networks & Groups
- Reputation is distributed social belief, not objective truth or one global score. Track audience/domain/reach/confidence.
- Fame = reach; notoriety = recognition for danger/scandal/crime; public image = dominant visible narrative.
- Rumors move through actual people, networks, institutions, witnesses, and communication channels. They may be true, partial, false, distorted, or outdated.
- Corrections do not automatically erase rumors.
- Social networks consist of strong/weak ties, bridges, hubs, gatekeepers, cliques, factions, and overlapping groups.
- Social capital is not a universal spendable currency.
- Group relationship is separate from individual relationships. Track belonging, identification, group Trust, legitimacy, Loyalty, duty, alienation, Resentment, Fear, cohesion, and informal standing.
- Formal membership/rank belongs to institutional systems; Ruleset 7 owns the subjective/social layer.

### 9. Friendship, Family & Rivalry
- Friendship emerges from repeated meaningful positive interaction, support, compatibility, shared interests, humor, hardship, and reliability.
- Family/kinship is objective; emotional closeness is separate. Family duty can exist with low Affection.
- Chosen family can be fully meaningful.
- Rivalry is persistent comparison/competition/opposition and is not automatically Hostility.
- Friendly, competitive, hostile, ideological, and one-sided rivalries are all valid.

### 10. Romance & Adult Sexual State
- Distinguish **Aesthetic Attraction, Emotional/Interpersonal Attraction, Romantic Interest, adult Sexual Attraction, Interpersonal Chemistry, and adult Sexual Chemistry**.
- Attraction is directional and may be asymmetric. Orientation defines possibility, not automatic attraction.
- Minors may have age-appropriate crushes, dating, jealousy, interest, and rejection. **Adult sexual/arousal/Seduction mechanics are 18+ only.**
- Flirting communicates or explores interest; politeness is not reciprocity.
- Adult Seduction may improve pacing, cue-reading, presentation, reciprocal tension, willingness to continue flirting, or temporary arousal **where genuine potential already exists**.
- Seduction cannot manufacture attraction, create consent, override refusal, or convert coercion into willingness.
- A clear romantic/sexual rejection ends the current pursuit. Reconsideration requires genuine changed circumstances, not persistence.
- Courtship explores interest, compatibility, Trust, expectations, and commitment. A date or relationship forms through mutual understanding/choice, not thresholds.
- Exclusivity is not assumed unless agreed.
- Established relationships continue evolving; love, Attachment, satisfaction, commitment, Trust, Sexual Chemistry, and compatibility remain distinct.
- Adult intimacy does not automatically repair conflict, create romance, or authorize future intimacy.
- **7.29 Boundaries, Consent & Compatibility remains intentionally undeveloped/skipped.** Existing rules still establish that arousal ≠ consent, prior consent ≠ permanent consent, and coercion ≠ freely given agreement.
- Jealousy is a response to perceived relational threat, not proof of love, betrayal, or ownership.
- Breakups may be mutual or one-sided; leaving does not require permission. Feelings can persist after breakup.
- Reconciliation requires renewed mutual choice and changed conditions; it never resets history.

### 11. Promises, Favors, Betrayal & Repair
- Promise, favor, gratitude, debt, and obligation are distinct.
- Not every kindness creates debt. Unsolicited help does not automatically create enforceable obligation.
- Favors must remain proportional; one favor never creates unlimited leverage.
- Life debts may exist culturally/personally but never imply ownership.
- Keep meaningful obligation records: parties, origin, terms, magnitude, status, conditions, witnesses/publicity if relevant.
- Broken promises differ by good-faith failure, negligence, and deliberate betrayal.
- Betrayal = violation of a meaningful expectation of Trust, Loyalty, secrecy, protection, commitment, or fair dealing.
- Intent matters, but does not erase impact.
- Rupture can be partial or full. Relationships may remain functional but damaged.
- Apology does not force forgiveness. Forgiveness does not equal restored Trust or reconciliation.
- Repair may require acknowledgment, restitution, changed behavior, transparency, and time.
- Reconciled relationships may retain scars or permanently lower expectations.

### 12. Leadership, Loyalty & Followership
- Formal authority, legitimacy, competence confidence, Trust, Respect, Loyalty, Fear, and obedience are separate.
- Rank does not guarantee genuine followership.
- Informal leaders may emerge without rank.
- Loyalty does not require blind obedience; dissent can coexist with strong Loyalty.
- Fear-based obedience may collapse when enforcement disappears.
- Command Confidence = belief that leadership can guide the current situation; it is separate from morale.
- Leadership style is contextual; no universal best style.
- Refusal, divided Loyalty, defection, desertion, informal factions, and leadership collapse can emerge causally.
- Leadership skill improves communication, delegation, coordination, morale support, and judgment; it does not create automatic obedience.

### 13. NPC-to-NPC Simulation, Routines & Access
- NPC social life continues off-screen using the same rules at lower resolution.
- Use relevance-weighted, event-driven simulation rather than simulating every NPC pair.
- Process existing ties, plausible new ties, and event-relevant pairs; prevent pair explosion.
- NPC relationships may form, deepen, weaken, rupture, reconcile, or become romantic without the player present.
- Do not create drama merely for entertainment; stable relationships may remain stable.
- NPCs exist in real time/place with routines involving sleep, work, missions, training, family, social time, and personal time.
- Availability is separate from physical location and depends on the requested interaction.
- Relationships can improve access but do not create unlimited access.
- Gatekeepers, appointments, privacy, restricted areas, rank, duty, work, missions, and family commitments remain real constraints.
- The player cannot summon NPCs or brute-force access through repeated visits/messages.

### 14. Social Consequences & Access
- Social state should change practical opportunity without becoming an unlock ladder.
- Access may include information, introductions, invitations, hospitality, shelter, mentorship, recommendations, assistance, private contact, or informal institutional opportunity.
- Trust, Affection, Respect, Loyalty, Obligation, reputation, networks, and group standing affect different access domains.
- High relationship does not grant every secret or formal clearance.
- Formal institutional permission remains owned by institutional systems.
- Social exclusion, ostracism, and blacklisting require actual network/reputation spread and may be local rather than global.
- Access may be temporary, conditional, revocable, partial, or restored after repair.
- No gift/compliment/favor grinding to unlock rare jutsu, homes, archives, or secrets.

### 15. Hidden State & Player-Facing Information
- Default narration is character-limited, not omniscient.
- Internal values, motives, secrets, NPC-to-NPC relationships, and hidden rolls remain hidden unless discovered.
- Show social state through behavior and change from baseline.
- Fair ambiguity requires observable clues; later revelations must fit prior state.
- Do not announce hidden rolls or narrate successful lies as lies.
- Track separately: objective truth, player knowledge, player belief/suspicion, NPC knowledge, NPC belief about what others know.
- OOC/debug/GM information never becomes player-character knowledge without an in-world path.

### 16. Social Learning & Progression
- Ruleset 7 defines what counts as meaningful practice; **Ruleset 1 owns XP, tiers, mastery, prerequisites, and growth math**.
- Social growth requires meaningful challenge, adaptation, feedback, observation, mentorship, cultural exposure, and/or reflection.
- No generic Social XP and no trivial repetition farming.
- Failure can teach only when the character can interpret useful feedback.
- Related skills may transfer limited learning, but direct practice dominates.
- Social mastery remains domain-specific. A master negotiator may still be a poor liar or leader.
- Trauma is not free training.
- Adult Seduction progression should reward cue-reading and graceful response to rejection, not persistence after refusal.

### 17. Persistence & Storage
- Persist the causal skeleton: stable NPC IDs, identity, major goals, relationships, directional dimensions, key memories, beliefs/knowledge, secrets, obligations, routines, access, reputation, group/network state, unresolved conflict, romantic structure, and major events.
- Keep both **current state** and **major causal history**. Do not rely only on snapshots or giant event logs.
- Compress routine repeated detail into summaries; preserve defining exceptions.
- Hidden GM state should remain separate from player-visible summaries.
- Already resolved hidden outcomes must persist and never reroll on reload/session change.
- Long time skips should simulate relevant social development rather than freezing everyone.
- Unknown, ungenerated, and missing data are different states.
- Generated filler must never override explicit established history.

### 18. Cross-Ruleset Ownership
- **Ruleset 1:** Attributes, Skills, XP, mastery, training, progression.
- **Ruleset 2:** probability, difficulty, opposed/hidden checks, degrees of success.
- **Ruleset 3:** chakra, jutsu, genjutsu, supernatural compulsion/memory/emotion/perception effects.
- **Ruleset 4:** wounds, pain, fatigue, medicine, recovery, physical captivity state.
- **Ruleset 7:** social meaning, relationships, beliefs, influence, reputation, memory, interpersonal consequence.
- Future mission/institution/economy/legal/travel/world systems own their formal mechanics; Ruleset 7 consumes their state and applies social consequences.
- Social belief never rewrites objective physical/mechanical reality.
- Social relationships may influence another system's decision but never silently own it.

### 19. Anti-Exploitation Rules
- No free reroll spam; repeated attempts require causal change.
- No Persuasion mind control.
- No Seduction-to-consent shortcut.
- No gift, compliment, favor, apology, reputation, access, or Social-XP farming.
- No relationship threshold vending machines.
- No Insight mind reading or Interrogation omniscience.
- No reputation telepathy or instant global blacklisting.
- No schedule summoning or harassment-as-access.
- No player romance reservation or protagonist attraction bonus.
- No social-skill bypass of progression, jutsu, medicine, law, security, rank, or institutional rules.
- No save/load rerolling of established social outcomes.
- Anti-exploit restrictions apply equally to NPCs and players.

### 20. Calibration Standard
Stress-test social rules across:
- low → exceptional skill;
- stranger → trusted friend → enemy;
- trivial → life-changing stakes;
- accurate → incomplete → false information;
- ordinary → extreme relationship states.

Core regression cases should include: reasonable request, firm refusal, Deception vs Insight, interrogation of unknown information, betrayal/repair, low-legitimacy leader, mutual attraction with incompatibility, adult Seduction after refusal, local rumor spread, off-screen NPC relationship development, long time skip, and injury/social cross-ruleset interaction.

A Ruleset 7 outcome is sound when **skill matters, relationships matter, context matters, information limits matter, agency remains intact, consequences persist, and no factor becomes a universal override.**
