# Ruleset 6 — Academy, Training, Missions & Careers

**Status:** Established Outline

## Purpose
Ruleset 6 governs the institutional and professional structure of shinobi life: Academy education, post-graduation placement, training opportunities, mission work, promotion, specialization, career development, service history, and retirement.

It does not replace the underlying mechanics of attributes, skills, resolution, chakra, jutsu, combat, injuries, medicine, or relationships. Instead, it determines how characters encounter and use those systems through education, training, missions, and careers.

## Design Direction
Rank, strength, institutional authority, professional reputation, and career specialization are related but distinct. Characters develop through actual time, training, missions, relationships, opportunities, and institutional decisions rather than through a simple level ladder. NPCs and the player use the same underlying world rules.
