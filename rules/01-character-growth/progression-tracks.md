# Progression-track index

Status: Established for the explicitly approved Chakra Control structure; Provisional for the other developed track proposals. This index routes to the owning rule; it does not independently approve its numbers.

| Domain | Record / progression model | Owning source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary skills | Generic I–VII framework where suitable, each with tier progress | [Skills](skills-mastery.md) |
| Chakra Control | Basic/Intermediate/Advanced/Expert I–V; Master I–VII (27 tiers) | [Control](../03-chakra-jutsu/chakra-control.md) |
| Chakra Output | Proposed Basic through Master I–V (25 tiers); separate derived throughput limits | [3.3](../03-chakra-jutsu/output-nature.md) |
| Each ordinary Nature Transformation | Proposed Basic through Master I–V (25 tiers), tracked independently | [3.4](../03-chakra-jutsu/output-nature.md) |
| Shape Transformation | Proposed Basic through Master I–V (25 tiers) | [3.5](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--35-shape-transformation) |
| Handsign Speed | Proposed Basic through Master I–V (25 tiers); physical sequencing, not total casting speed | [3.9](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--39-jutsu-execution-hand-seals-and-casting-speed) |
| Medical Ninjutsu | Proposed Basic through Master I–V (25 tiers); medical knowledge remains required | [3.13](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--313-medical-ninjutsu-framework) |
| Sensory Ninjutsu | Proposed Basic through Master I–V (25 tiers); each sensory channel has defined scope | [3.14](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--314-sensory-ninjutsu-and-chakra-detection) |
| Fūinjutsu | Proposed Basic through Master I–V (25 tiers); design and execution are separate capabilities | [3.15](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--315-fūinjutsu-sealing-and-barrier-techniques) |
| General ability / dōjutsu / Senjutsu proficiency | Optional 0–100 when broad manipulation needs its own record; not mandatory for passive traits | [3.18–24](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--318-universal-special-ability-framework) |
| Individual jutsu mastery | Technique-specific 0–100 after functional acquisition | [3.8](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--38-individual-jutsu-mastery) |
| Jutsu acquisition / research | Persistent learning or research state; not interchangeable with mastery | [3.7](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--37-jutsu-learning-and-acquisition), [3.27](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--327-jutsu-creation-research-and-invention) |
| Chakra Flow | Application family using existing skills and technique mastery, not a mandatory extra universal skill | [3.17](../03-chakra-jutsu/jutsu-special-abilities.md#provisional--317-chakra-flow-reinforcement-and-chakra-enhanced-combat) |

Generic Tier V, Basic V, Master I, ability proficiency 80 and jutsu mastery 80 do not describe equivalent capability. Rank-generation expectations and generic tier growth tables must identify their track before use.

No automatic numeric mapping between tracks exists. Prerequisites, growth coefficients and the Ruleset 2 Skill input need track-specific calibration under [issue #1](https://github.com/SirSparxs/Naruto-Shinobi-Life/issues/1) and [issue #2](https://github.com/SirSparxs/Naruto-Shinobi-Life/issues/2).
