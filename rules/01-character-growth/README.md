# Ruleset 1 — Character Attributes, Skills, Potential and Growth

Status: Established framework; Provisional calibration; Open implementation details.

This ruleset answers what a character can fundamentally do, what they have learned, how familiar they are with specific techniques, and how those capabilities change over time.

- [Attributes and derived values](attributes.md)
- [Skills and individual mastery](skills-mastery.md)
- [Specialized progression tracks](progression-tracks.md)
- [Growth, potential and generation](growth-generation.md)
- [Provisional calibration tables](calibration.md)

Source: **Character Growth Ruleset** (S1 in [source index](../../migration/source-index.md)), especially the explicit attribute revision, accepted scope refinement, and Components A–G. The full available discussion is preserved as reference excerpts.

Detailed combat, jutsu effects, promotions, medical treatment and clan abilities belong to other systems. [Issue #1](https://github.com/SirSparxs/Naruto-Shinobi-Life/issues/1) tracks calibration; [issue #2](https://github.com/SirSparxs/Naruto-Shinobi-Life/issues/2) tracks translating tiered skills into resolution capability.
